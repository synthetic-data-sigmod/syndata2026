SynData@SIGMOD 2026
Workshop on Synthetic Data Generation, Evaluation, and Applications for Data-Centric AI

HOME SPEAKERS AND PANELISTS CALL FOR PAPERS WORKSHOP SCHEDULE

Workshop Format: The workshop will feature four main components designed to foster collaboration and knowledge exchange in the field of synthetic data generation:

  • Keynote Talks: Distinguished researchers from academia and industry will deliver keynote presentations on cutting-edge topics such as LLM-based data synthesis, responsible synthetic data generation, and evaluation frameworks. Each keynote will be followed by Q&A sessions to engage the audience.
  • Invited Talks: Expert speakers will present focused talks on specific aspects of synthetic data systems, including scalability, privacy preservation, and real-world applications in domains like healthcare, finance, and enterprise analytics.
  • Paper Presentations: Accepted peer-reviewed papers will be presented in organized sessions, showcasing novel research and practical systems. Papers will be presented in both short and long formats, highlighting the latest advances in synthetic data generation and evaluation.
  • Panel Discussion: A panel of experts from diverse backgrounds will discuss "Responsible Synthetic Data in Open and Enterprise Research," exploring challenges related to privacy, fairness, bias mitigation, and the future of synthetic data pipelines. The panel will encourage audience participation through curated and open questions.
  • Poster and Demo Sessions: Researchers will have opportunities to present early-stage research and prototype systems in interactive poster sessions, fostering networking and collaborative discussions.
